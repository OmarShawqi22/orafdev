What it is

Attoor.CEGS is a construction, engineering and general services company that delivers residential, commercial and renovation projects. We built its website: the page a client opens to decide whether this is the contractor they call.

Who it serves

Property owners, developers and businesses looking for a contractor they can trust, whether they read in Arabic or in English.

What we built

  • Full English and Arabic versions, with a right-to-left layout for Arabic
  • Light and dark modes, one tap from the menu
  • A hero slideshow of real site photos
  • An admin dashboard where the company adds its own projects, without a developer
  • A project gallery filtered by residential, commercial and renovation work
  • A company profile with the figures clients ask about
  • A contact form and direct email
  • Smooth scroll animations on a layout built for phones first
