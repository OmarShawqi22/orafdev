What it is

A car rental business runs on two screens: the website where customers book, and the dashboard where the team manages the fleet. CarHub covers both.

For customers

  • Browse the fleet with live rates and offers
  • Car pages with full details and reviews
  • Book, follow and edit trips from one account
  • Favourites, profile, light and dark mode, and WhatsApp chat

For the team

  • Bookings, cancellations and car availability
  • Fleet, services and catalog management
  • Customers, reviews and staff accounts
  • Payments, invoices, receipts, promotions and reports
